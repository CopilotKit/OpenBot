## A2UI

- The extension contract is `InferenceFormatFactory.createFormat` and `InferenceFormat.createParser`. Python and Dart expose the same prompt/parser division. [TypeScript](https://github.com/a2ui-project/a2ui/blob/289923cb2e5a2ba72cab21f0df675a55cc5b657f/typescript/agents/a2ui_agent/src/inference-format.ts#L21-L47) · [Python](https://github.com/a2ui-project/a2ui/blob/289923cb2e5a2ba72cab21f0df675a55cc5b657f/python/a2ui_agent/src/a2ui/inference_format.py#L32-L65) · [Dart](https://github.com/a2ui-project/a2ui/blob/289923cb2e5a2ba72cab21f0df675a55cc5b657f/dart/a2ui_agent/lib/src/inference_format.dart#L21-L54)
- Catalog loading, negotiation and example validation are in `CatalogConfig`, `resolveCatalogs`, and `A2uiGenerator.createProcessor`. `A2uiRequestProcessor` owns the negotiated context, state selection, prompt and parser facade. [Catalog config](https://github.com/a2ui-project/a2ui/blob/289923cb2e5a2ba72cab21f0df675a55cc5b657f/typescript/agents/a2ui_agent/src/processor/catalog-config.ts) · [Negotiation](https://github.com/a2ui-project/a2ui/blob/289923cb2e5a2ba72cab21f0df675a55cc5b657f/typescript/agents/a2ui_agent/src/utils/catalog-resolver.ts#L102) · [Generator](https://github.com/a2ui-project/a2ui/blob/289923cb2e5a2ba72cab21f0df675a55cc5b657f/typescript/agents/a2ui_agent/src/processor/generator.ts#L61-L97) · [Processor](https://github.com/a2ui-project/a2ui/blob/289923cb2e5a2ba72cab21f0df675a55cc5b657f/typescript/agents/a2ui_agent/src/processor/processor.ts#L38)
- Express builds its prompt and optional skills from the catalog, then decompiles canonical examples into Express. `ExpressParser` unwraps text and calls `ExpressCompiler`. `ExpressResponseStream` handles text chunks and staged messages. The integration guide places model calls and retries in application code. ## Gemini and Jev

- Identity, beta REST endpoint, question and answer schemas, SDK support, authentication and pricing. [Model](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/models/gemini-decision-1) · [Decisions guide](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/decisions-api) · [REST schema](https://aistudio.google.com/api-reference/decisions/create-decision) · [Gemini keys](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/api-key) · [Decision pricing](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/pricing#gemini-decision) · [Flash-Lite model and configuration](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/models/gemini-3.5-flash-lite) · [Flash-Lite pricing](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/pricing#gemini-3.5-flash-lite)
- Jev formulates questions in `planQuestions` or in its screen grammar. `askJev` sends them to TypeSafe `systemOne` using `jev-latest` by default, with a configurable Gev-compatible endpoint. [Questions and plan](https://github.com/dglazkov/jev2ui/blob/b0e38743dcefaa2fc315737229a474ba5be7b067/src/server/plan.ts#L87) · [Screen grammar](https://github.com/dglazkov/jev2ui/blob/b0e38743dcefaa2fc315737229a474ba5be7b067/grammar/screen.md) · [Backend](https://github.com/dglazkov/jev2ui/blob/b0e38743dcefaa2fc315737229a474ba5be7b067/src/server/models.ts#L185)
- `runHybrid` emits a deterministic skeleton and fills data from a separate Gemini call. `runMock` reads the grammar, builds component trees, and sends surface messages directly. Its main demo uses custom component sets, its own graph checks and renderer, and a `defineComponent` extension. Its compare view does use the upstream v0.9 Basic catalog and Lit.
